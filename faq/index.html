Common Questions

Do I need a Yukon fishing license?

Yes. All anglers need a valid Yukon fishing license. We can help you obtain one before your trip or direct you to the government issuing office.

What equipment is provided?

All rods, reels, tackle, boat, motor, fuel, and safety gear are provided. You need to bring warm clothing, rain gear, and a license.

What species can I catch?

Lake trout, northern pike, Arctic grayling, and seasonal salmon runs are all possible depending on location and time of year.

How much does a fishing trip cost?

Half-day lake trips start from $300 per person. Full-day river trips and multi-day expeditions are higher. Contact us for current rates.

Can children come on fishing trips?

Yes. Fishing is family-friendly. Children 12 and under fish free with a paying adult on most trips. We recommend ages 6+ for half-day trips.

What is catch and release vs catch and keep?

Yukon fishing regulations vary by species and location. Some waters are catch-and-release only. Your guide will explain what applies to your chosen location.
