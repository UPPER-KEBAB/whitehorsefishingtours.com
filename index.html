Whitehorse Fishing Tours

Fishing charters and guided trips from Whitehorse, Yukon.

The lakes and rivers around Whitehorse offer some of the best freshwater fishing in Northern Canada. Lake trout, northern pike, Arctic grayling, and salmon all inhabit these waters. Our guided fishing trips put you on the fish with experienced guides who know the productive spots and the seasonal patterns.

Our Services

Full-Day River Fishing

8-hour guided float or wade trip on Yukon rivers.

Ice Fishing (Winter)
