Before the first cast
More questions ↗
Is a catch guaranteed?＋
No outcome is promised by this guide. Ask the provider what the trip includes regardless of fishing results.
Does the guide supply my licence?＋
Confirm this directly and check current official requirements yourself. A guided trip should not be assumed to include a licence.
Can I keep what I catch?＋
That depends on current rules for the water, species and method, plus the operator’s policy. Check before the trip rather than deciding on the water.
