Our Fishing Trips

Our fishing trips are led by experienced Yukon angling guides. They know which lakes hold lake trout in June, where the grayling rise in August, and where the pike ambush prey year-round. All trips include boat, motor, fuel, rods, reels, and tackle. You need a Yukon fishing license — we can help you obtain one.

What's Included

  • Experienced Yukon fishing guides with local knowledge
  • All equipment provided — rods, reels, tackle, boat, motor
  • Lake, river, and ice fishing options
  • Multi-day expeditions to remote fishing camps
  • Target species: lake trout, northern pike, Arctic grayling, salmon
  • Fishing license assistance
  • Catch-and-keep or catch-and-release as regulations allow
